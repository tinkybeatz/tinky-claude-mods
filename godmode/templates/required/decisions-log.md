# Decisions log

Product and technical decisions for {{projectName}}, with the reason behind each, so
nobody has to rediscover why the code is the way it is. Started {{date}}.

- Append only. New entries go at the bottom; past entries are never rewritten. A decision
  that changes gets a new entry that names the one it replaces.
- A decision is logged only once the person who owns it has said yes. Until then it
  stays under "Open questions".
- One decision per entry, with its reason. The reason is the point.
- A large architectural decision gets a full record in `{{adrDir}}`, and a one-line entry
  here that links to it.
